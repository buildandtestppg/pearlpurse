📱 Install as an app: iOS Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen · Android Chrome → Install app. Full-screen, own icon, works like a native wallet.
🔑 Non-custodial
Seed encrypted on-device (PBKDF2-600k · AES-256-GCM). No accounts, no servers holding keys, no tracking.
🌀 Rotating taproot addresses
BIP86 bech32m with automatic rotation — payments land on fresh addresses. Privacy by default.
✍️ Sign & verify proofs
Schnorr proof-of-address built in — what OTC desks ask for. Verify anyone's proof in-app.
📈 Honest fees & network state
Real per-block fee curve, live chain status, and an advisory that tells you exactly what a send costs.
🔗 Payment links
pearl:pay URIs and QR codes — invoices that open pre-filled in the wallet.
🛡️ Verifiable builds
Every release builds reproducibly; SHA-256 checksums published and checkable against the live site. Strict CSP, cross-review on every change.
