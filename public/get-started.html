Why PearlPurse

🔑 Non-custodial by construction Your seed is encrypted (PBKDF2-600k · AES-256-GCM) in your browser storage. No servers hold keys, no accounts, no tracking.
🌀 Rotating taproot addresses BIP86 bech32m addresses with automatic rotation — payments land on fresh addresses, privacy by default.
✍️ Sign & verify proofs Prove address ownership with a Schnorr signature — the standard OTC desks ask for. Verify anyone's proof in-app.
📈 Honest fees & network state Real block-target fee curve (next-block / ~2 / ~5 blocks), live chain status, and an advisory that tells you exactly what a send costs.

Install on your phone (60 seconds)

  • iOS Safari: open pearlpurse.vercel.app → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android Chrome: open the site → tap Install app.
  • Create a wallet, write the 12 words on paper (never a screenshot), done.

How to get PRL

1 · SafeTrade (exchange) Withdraw PRL to your PearlPurse address. Send a small test amount first. The in-wallet Get PRL guide walks you through it with a clipboard sanity-check.
2 · OTC desks Ask for settlement to your address and hand over a signed proof-of-address (✍️ Sign in-app).
3 · Another Pearl wallet Any bech32m-capable sender works.
